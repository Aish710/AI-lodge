# Test card (read aloud, one page)

**Before you start** (read this out)
"I am testing an app, not you. You can stop at any time. I will note your answers without your name. **Is it OK if I note your answers and quote them anonymously?**" (write yes or no: `quote_ok`)

**Your task**
"Plan a 3-day trip using this app, with no help from me. Use your own picks, or the example if you like." (Observer: time from landing to the first plan. Do not coach.)

**Step 2: before the comparison** (three hotels with nightly rates only)
- "Which would you book?"
- "How confident are you, 1 to 5?"

**Step 3: after the comparison**
- "Which would you book now?"
- "How confident are you now, 1 to 5?"
- "Did the comparison change your choice?" (yes / no) and "Why, in a few words?"
- Observer: from the app, was the best-value hotel different from the cheapest-rate hotel? (yes / no)

**Step 4**
- "How long would this planning take you without the app, in minutes?" (their own estimate)

**Step 5: rate 1 to 5** (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)
1. It was clear what to do.
2. I trust the result.
3. I would use something like this.
4. The AI part helped.
5. It was easy to find the AI.

**Last question (free text):** "What confused you most?"

Observer: record **no names, emails or phone numbers**. Use ids P1 to P10.
