## Ambiguous cases and how they were resolved

- **market**: Default Food & drink (food markets). A craft or souvenir market is overridden to Shopping below.
- **district**: Only Akihabara (an electronics and retail district), so Shopping.
- **mall**: Only Jewel Changi (a mall with a garden attraction): primary theme Shopping.
- **neighbourhood**: Default Sightseeing (heritage streets). A shopping street is overridden below.
- **architecture**: Dongdaemun Design Plaza is a landmark building (it also has shops): primary theme Sightseeing.
- **Central Market Kuala Lumpur** (override to Shopping): A handicraft and souvenir market, not a food market.
- **Insadong** (override to Shopping): A street known for craft, art and tea shops; its category is neighbourhood.
