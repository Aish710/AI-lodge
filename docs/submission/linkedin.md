I built **Fitinerary** for the AI Lodge hackathon: a Tokyo trip-planning app that chooses the hotel and itinerary together instead of treating them as separate decisions.

The biggest challenges were making the optimisation results honest, handling unknown sample data safely, and ensuring evidence from tests/evaluations could not be mistaken for something more complete than it was. I addressed those with labelled limitations, validator checks, generated evidence artifacts, and explicit warnings when data or results are stale.

What I would improve next: verified live data, more cities, public holiday rules, walking-limit enforcement, and a live AI quality evaluation once I have approved API usage.
